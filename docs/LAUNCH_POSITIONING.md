# Elexa AI Trading — Launch Positioning

## Product Position

Elexa AI Trading should launch as an AI market research, risk management, journaling and simulated strategy-testing platform.

It should not be positioned as:

- A live trading bot
- A guaranteed profit system
- A signal service
- A financial adviser
- A broker or exchange

## Core Launch Promise

Research smarter. Test before you trade.

Elexa helps users slow down, inspect market context, understand risk, test strategy ideas in simulation and keep a disciplined decision journal before risking real capital elsewhere.

## Launch-Mode Boundaries

Launch mode must remain clear:

- Simulated execution only
- No real-money order placement
- No personal investment recommendations
- No guaranteed returns
- No claims that AI can predict markets
- No regulated financial advice

## Homepage Pillars

1. AI Market Research
2. Risk & Portfolio Tools
3. Strategy Simulation
4. Decision Journal

## Safer Language To Use

Use:

- Research dashboard
- Strategy simulation
- Simulated portfolio
- Trade journal
- Risk review
- Market scanner
- Educational research
- AI-assisted analysis
- Not financial advice

Avoid:

- Guaranteed profit
- Passive income from trading
- Copy our trades
- Buy/sell signal service
- Live auto-trading
- Beat the market
- Risk-free returns

## Version 1 Launch Checklist

### Must Have

- Homepage positioned as research + risk + simulation
- Pricing page removes live-trading promises
- Disclaimer visible from homepage and pricing
- Dashboard works with demo/sample data
- Clear onboarding path for beginners
- Free plan active
- Paid plans connected only after Stripe environment variables are verified

### Should Have

- How It Works page
- About page
- Security page
- Roadmap page
- FAQ section
- Demo screenshots or walkthrough video
- Email capture/waitlist

### Later

- Broker/exchange integrations
- Real execution review
- Legal review before any real-money trading
- FCA/UK financial-promotion review if marketed to UK users
- Partner route with authorised firms if needed

## Final Launch Angle

Elexa AI Trading is for traders, learners and analysts who want a better decision process: research the market, check the risk, test the strategy and review the outcome before risking real capital elsewhere.
